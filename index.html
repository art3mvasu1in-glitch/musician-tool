Who we ?

We're a new startup built to help musicians and sound engineers. How exactly do we help?

  1. We provide a database of chords and lyrics — accessible offline, transposable to any key,
  2. With the ability to add your own notes.
  3. You can also compose custom song lists for concerts, services, and rehearsals.
  4. And with a built-in metronome, practicing and rehearsing together becomes easier and more precise than ever.
